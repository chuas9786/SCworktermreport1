Work Term Report

A reflection on my experience as a IT Service Support COOP at City of Guelph, where I gained hands-on experience in an IT and developed skills that will shape my career in computer science.

January – August 2025
Guelph, Ontario
City of Guelph

Introduction

Welcome to my work term report. Through this website, I hope to share the valuable experiences and lessons I gained during my time at the city of Guelph. You'll discover how this role challenged me to grow as a developer, introduced me to industry practices, and helped me clarify my career direction in computer science.

During this four-month term, I worked as a member of the IT Service Desk support in the IT department of the city of guleph, contributing to the assistance of problems in the technology and systems of the city. This experience bridged the gap between academic theory and real-world application in meaningful ways.

About the Employer

City of Guelph is a municipal government organization located in Guelph that provides public services to its residents. The city manages important community services such as transportation, water and wastewater systems, emergency services, parks and recreation, and digital government operations. The Information Technology department supports these services by maintaining the city’s computer systems, networks, cybersecurity, software, and technical support assistance. The IT Service Desk team acts as the first point of contact for employees who need help with hardware, software, accounts, and other technology-related issues.

The company operated on a hybrid schedule for coops with 1 week on and 1 week off. When working online we were expected to be in queue taking calls with the cisco phone system, and managing the tickets by delegating them to the right times or providing support if it was a problem that we could solve in our position such as software installs or password resets. In the office there is more work that is required on top of the calls and managing tickets which included cable organization for different workstations that required it or going to different parts of the city such as the parametric/ fire station, to help with setting up multifactor authentication to new hires.

The area of computing science related to this co-op role is Information Technology Support. As a member of the IT Service Desk team I was about to gain experience with troubleshooting desktop and mobile devices, setting up computers, installing software, managing user accounts, and supporting Windows based systems. The role also involves customer service, problem solving, and communication skills, which are essential in IT operations and technical support careers.

Learning Goals

The objectives I set for this work term.

Learn the proper way to assist customers in a corporate setting. Be able to convey technical words in the process in a simple way for the client to understand clearly

This experence allowed me to learn that providing good customer service is not only about solving technical problems, but also about being patient, professional, and understanding the client’s concerns. I had opportunities to assist users with troubleshooting issues, answering questions, and guiding them through solutions step by step. I found it challenging at first to avoid using technical words, especially when explaining computer or software problems. Over time I was able to get a grasp of their technological proficiency and improved using simpler language to make sure the customer understand my solution

be able to break down and understand problems when asked something by a client. To be able to understand all the information I need to solve a problem

Throughout this experience, I learned that understanding a problem fully is one of the most important parts of technical support. When clients explained their issues, I practiced asking clear questions, listening carefully, and identifying important details such as error messages, recent changes, or steps already attempted. This helped me better analyze situations instead of rushing to conclusions. At times, some problems were difficult to understand right away, especially when clients were unsure how to describe the issue. Over time, I became more confident in guiding conversations and breaking larger problems into smaller parts to find the root cause. I also learned the importance of staying patient and organized while troubleshooting.

To be a productive and positive member to the team

Throughout my placement, I learned that teamwork is very important in IT because many technical issues require communication and cooperation between team members. I worked alongside co-workers to troubleshoot problems, share ideas, and find solutions efficiently. Whenever I faced challenges that I could not solve on my own, I learned to ask questions and seek guidance from more experienced team members. This helped me improve my technical knowledge while also building confidence in working with others. I also focused on maintaining a positive attitude, being respectful, and supporting my teammates whenever possible. By staying organized, completing assigned tasks, and communicating clearly, I was able to contribute to the team’s overall success. I learned that being a good team member means being reliable, open to feedback, and willing to help others when needed.

Learn to be more organized and work faster on the job

During my time as a coop, I learned the importance of time management and staying organized in a busy work environment. A major part of this was learning how to properly use the ticketing system to manage support requests. I learned how to create, update, prioritize, and close tickets while documenting important information clearly and accurately. Using the ticketing system helped me stay organized by keeping track of ongoing issues, deadlines, and completed tasks. I also handled multiple responsibilities such as responding to support requests, troubleshooting technical issues, and following up with users. At first, balancing several tickets at once was challenging, but over time I improved my ability to prioritize urgent requests and manage my workload more efficiently. By keeping detailed notes in tickets and following structured troubleshooting steps, I became faster at identifying and solving common problems.

To be able to confidently do tickets on my own without much assistance from coworkers

I learned how to properly use the ticketing system to manage and document support requests. I became more comfortable creating, updating, prioritizing, and closing tickets while ensuring that all important details were recorded clearly. At the beginning of my placement, I often relied on co-workers for guidance when troubleshooting unfamiliar issues. However, as I gained more experience, I became better at researching solutions, following troubleshooting steps, and resolving issues on my own. Working independently on tickets helped me improve my critical thinking and decision-making skills. I learned how to gather the necessary information from clients, identify possible causes of issues, and apply solutions efficiently. I also became more confident communicating with users and providing updates throughout the support process. Learn to be more organized and work faster on the job

Job Description

During my co-op placement as an IT Service Desk Coop with the City of Guelph, my main responsibility was providing technical support to city employees through tickets submitted into the ticketing system, calls from the phone queue, and managing walk in issues.. I assisted users with troubleshooting hardware and software issues, setting up computers and mobile devices, installing applications, resetting passwords, and resolving network or account access problems. I also helped document support tickets and worked with other IT staff to ensure issues were resolved efficiently. The job required both technical and interpersonal skills. Technical skills included troubleshooting Windows computers, using ticketing systems, installing software, and supporting printers and mobile devices. Strong customer service, teamwork, organization, and communication skills were also important because I regularly assisted users with different levels of technical knowledge.

Conclusions

In conclusion, my co-op experience with the City of Guelph provided valuable insight into the daily operations of an IT Service Desk and the important role technology plays in supporting municipal services. Through this position, I developed technical troubleshooting abilities, improved my communication, problem solving, and customer service skills, and gained practical experience working in a professional IT environment.
